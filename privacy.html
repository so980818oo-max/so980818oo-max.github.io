MUSE Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MUSE (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our AI-assisted content studio and related TikTok publishing features.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information you provide (such as email).
  • TikTok authorization data when you connect TikTok via OAuth, including access tokens, refresh tokens, open ID, and basic profile fields granted by you (for example display name and avatar), according to the scopes you approve.
  • Content data such as videos, captions, hashtags, and publish settings you submit for upload or posting.
  • Usage and technical data such as timestamps, API status responses, device/browser type, and error logs needed to operate and debug the Service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session.
  • To upload drafts or publish content to TikTok on your behalf using official TikTok APIs.
  • To provide status updates, troubleshooting, and customer support.
  • To improve reliability, security, and product features.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing

We share data with:

  • TikTok, as required to perform login and content posting actions you request.
  • Service providers that host infrastructure or process data on our behalf under contractual safeguards.
  • Authorities when required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Retention

We retain information only as long as needed to provide the Service, fulfill the purposes described above, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. You may disconnect TikTok authorization at any time; we will delete or stop using tokens that are no longer required for the Service.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect tokens and content. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure.

6. Your Choices

  • Revoke TikTok access in your TikTok account settings.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by contacting us.

7. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

8. Children

MUSE is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a revised “Last updated” date.
